The Slapshot.Media Model

All team stats come from NHL.com. We use real goals scored and allowed, not expected goals (xG). What actually went in the net.

1
Away goals

(Away team's road goals for per game + Home team's home goals against per game) ÷ 2

2
Home goals

(Home team's home goals for per game + Away team's road goals against per game) ÷ 2

3
Win %

Team's projected goals ÷ total goals in the game. Higher % is our pick.
